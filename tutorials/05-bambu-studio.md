# 🖨️ HueForge to Bambu Studio: Complete Beginner's Guide

## Introduction

You've created your artwork in HueForge, selected your filament colors, and adjusted your layer transitions. Now it's time to turn your digital artwork into a physical 3D print!

This tutorial walks through exporting your HueForge project, importing it into Bambu Studio, configuring filament changes, and preparing your printer.

**What You'll Learn:**
- How to export your HueForge artwork
- How to import your model into Bambu Studio
- How to configure filament colors
- How to set up filament changes
- How to verify layer heights
- How to preview and prepare your print

## Step 1: Export Your Project From HueForge

Once you're satisfied with your HueForge preview:

1. Save your HueForge project.
2. Locate the export options.
3. Export your printable model as an STL or another supported format.
4. Save the filament change instructions generated by HueForge.
5. Keep your exported files together in a project folder.

### Recommended File Organization

```text
My-HueForge-Project/
├── original-image.png
├── hueforge-project.hfp
├── exported-model.stl
├── filament-changes.txt
└── finished-print.jpg
```

The exact exported filenames and available formats depend on your HueForge version.

**Beginner Tip:** Don't rely on the STL file alone. STL contains model geometry, not your complete filament color-change instructions.

## Step 2: Import Your Model Into Bambu Studio

1. Open Bambu Studio.
2. Select your printer model.
3. Confirm the correct nozzle size.
4. Import your exported HueForge model.
5. Place the model on the build plate.
6. Check that the model dimensions fit your printer.

### Important

HueForge artwork is typically designed to print flat.

Avoid rotating the model vertically unless your specific project requires a different orientation.
