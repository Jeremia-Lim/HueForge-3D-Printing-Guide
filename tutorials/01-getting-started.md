# Getting Started with HueForge

Welcome to my beginner-friendly HueForge tutorial!

This guide walks through the process of converting a 2D image into multi-color 3D printed artwork.

## What You'll Need

- A 3D printer (I'm using a Bambu Lab printer)
- HueForge software
- Bambu Studio
- PLA filament in multiple colors
- A photograph or digital illustration 
## Step 1: Choose Your Image

Choose a clear image with good contrast and recognizable details.

**Beginner Tip:** Images with simple backgrounds and distinct colors are often easier to work with.

## Step 2: Import Your Image

1. Open HueForge.
2. Import your selected image.
3. Adjust the dimensions.
4. Review the initial preview.

## Step 3: Select Your Filament Colors

1. Open the filament selection controls.
2. Choose filament profiles that match your available colors.
3. Review the transmission distance (TD) values.
4. Experiment with color combinations.

## Step 4: Adjust Layer Transitions

Use HueForge's layer controls to adjust where filament changes occur.

Observe how these adjustments affect the preview.

## Step 5: Export to Bambu Studio

1. Export your model from HueForge.
2. Save the layer-change instructions.
3. Import the model into Bambu Studio.
4. Configure the required filament changes.
5. Slice and review the preview before printing.
